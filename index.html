Distributed Systems & Multi-LLM

IntelliRoute: A Distributed Control Plane for Multi-LLM Orchestration

Anukrithi Myadala, Larry Nguyen, James Pham, Surbhi Singh

SJSU CMPE 273 — Enterprise Distributed Systems, Spring 2026

Most teams that ship LLM features call a provider SDK directly, which fails when adding a second provider. IntelliRoute serves as a distributed control plane for multi-LLM orchestration, managing routing, quotas, and fallback mechanisms.

Observability & Self-Healing

Mesh Control: A Self-Healing Microservice Mesh with Bounded LLM-Driven RCA

Vineet Kumar, Samved Sandeep Joshi, Girith Choudhary

SJSU CMPE 273 — Enterprise Distributed Systems, Spring 2026

An autonomous Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) agent for a Docker Compose mesh of eight gRPC microservices. Subscribes to a NATS telemetry bus, computes per-service metrics over a sliding window, and triggers bounded LLM-driven root-cause analysis (RCA) for recovery.

Observability & Multi-Agent

TraceAggregator: A Distributed Observability Platform for Multi-Agent LLM Pipelines

Aishwarya Madhave, Nihad Karathodath Parambil, Saransh Soni, Vandan Shah

SJSU Master's in Software Engineering

Multi-agent LLM pipelines fail in ways traditional tracing tools cannot surface. TraceAggregator is a distributed observability platform specifically designed to capture agent dependencies, trace agent state transitions, and diagnose pipeline failures.

Distributed Systems & Traffic Shaping

Geo-Distributed Rate Limiting with AI-Driven Traffic Shaping

Atharva Prasanna Mokashi, Nikhilraj Singh, Prathamesh Sawant, Yashashav Devalapalli Kamalraj

SJSU Master's in Software Engineering

A highly resilient rate limiter combining G-Counter CRDTs for cross-region synchronization, regional failover, a Prometheus-driven control plane, and adaptive traffic-shaping. Features live RPS forecasting (EWMA/Holt-Winters) to dynamically adapt rate limiting policies in Redis.

Generative AI & Federated Clusters

Distributed AI Gateway: A Fault-Tolerant, Routing-Based Compute Cluster for Federated LLM Inference

Divya Rajasekar, Conlyn Pattison, Shivansh Chhabra, Abhinand Vijayakumar Binsu

SJSU Computer Engineering Department

An innovative, cost-conscious inference system aggregating idle heterogeneous commodity laptops over a Tailscale VPN mesh. Implements intelligent prompt routing, live resource monitoring, and automated leader election/failover evaluated across a physical four-laptop cluster.

About Me

I lead AI transformation for IBM Software Support, turning LLMs and GenAI into real outcomes at enterprise scale. My work focuses on improving productivity, accelerating time to resolution, and increasing customer satisfaction by embedding safe, observable AI directly into support workflows.

I have spent over two decades at IBM building and scaling engineering platforms, including AIOps and cloud native systems with OpenTelemetry. I focus on building high performing teams, measuring outcomes, and delivering systems that create lasting value.

I have been teaching at San Jose State University since 2011, where I cover enterprise software, distributed systems, and AI. I am also a strong believer in giving back, and regularly mentor and coach students and early career professionals.
